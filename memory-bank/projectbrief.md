# Project Brief

## Purpose

Personal portfolio website for **Sanket Talaviya**, a Full Stack Engineer. The portfolio is a **product-engineering profile** designed to generate interviews, opportunities, and conversations with technical hiring managers.

## Target Audience

- Remote startups
- SaaS companies
- AI-enabled companies
- Product engineering teams
- Technical founders
- Engineering managers

## Primary Goals

1. Maximize recruiter, founder, and engineering manager confidence within 60 seconds of landing.
2. Position Sanket as an engineer who independently owns and delivers complex product systems — not someone who just completes tasks.
3. Drive hiring conversations through direct contact links (email, LinkedIn, GitHub).

## Scope

- Single-page scrolling experience with anchor-based navigation.
- Seven sections: Hero, Impact Highlights, Featured Systems, Engineering Interests, Experience Timeline, Skills, Contact.
- Dark-only theme (no light mode).
- Static, pre-rendered content — no dynamic data, no CMS.
- No sub-pages aside from the main landing page and a sitemap.

## High-Level Features

- Full name and positioning statement hero section (80-90vh).
- Impact metrics grid showing measurable outcomes.
- Four featured system "mini case studies" with problem, architecture, responsibilities, challenges, outcomes, and business impact.
- Engineering interests list communicating direction rather than technologies.
- Vertical career timeline with role/company/period.
- Grouped skill categories (no ratings or progress bars).
- Contact links: Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, downloadable Resume PDF.
- Scroll-triggered fade+slide reveal animations (300ms, IntersectionObserver-based).
- Responsive design across mobile (640px), tablet (1024px), and desktop (1100px+).
- SEO: JSON-LD Person schema, Open Graph, Twitter metadata, sitemap, robots.txt.

## Engineering Philosophy

- **Business impact first.** Every section and project exists to build hiring confidence.
- **Content over decoration.** Typography, layout, and spacing carry the design — not visual gimmicks.
- **All content visible.** No accordions, carousels, tabs, modals, or popups.
- **Clarity over cleverness.** Visitors understand who Sanket is and what he's built quickly.
- **Measurable outcomes.** Project descriptions tie technical work to business results.
- **Technology is supporting evidence, never the headline.**
